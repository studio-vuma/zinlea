Zinlea Night · Partnerships

Overnight support for care providers.

Zinlea Night partners with a limited number of organisations and private residences seeking discreet overnight clinical support in London.

Partnership enquiry
Residential & clinical partnerships

For care homes and residences.

We work with a small number of carefully selected residential and clinical partners, providing overnight escalation support between 8pm and 8am.

  • Complements existing care teams with overnight clinical decision support and reassurance
  • Triage, remote consultation and prescribing where clinically appropriate
  • Governance-led escalation aligned with your existing care model
Organisational partnerships

For family offices and organisations.

We partner with family offices, leadership teams and small organisations seeking private overnight support for their people and the communities they look after.

How partnership fees work.

Partnership fees are monthly and tailored to your requirements, following an introductory conversation to ensure clinical suitability.

First

Introductory conversation

We learn about your setting, your residents or people, and where nights feel stretched.

Then

Clinical suitability

We confirm that our remote overnight model is right for your setting.

Monthly

Tailored partnership

A clear monthly arrangement, reviewed with you over time.
