What to expect

You speak directly to a senior nurse.

When you contact Zinlea Night, you speak with an experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioner. We listen carefully, ask the right questions and give clear clinical guidance.

Unlike a standard out-of-hours service, our focus is continuity and reassurance. When in-person care is needed, we help you understand your options and escalate appropriately. Everything is confidential.

For care providers

Overnight support for care homes and residences.

Zinlea Night works with a small number of carefully selected care homes, residences, family offices and organisations, providing overnight escalation support between 8pm and 8am.

  • Complements existing care teams with overnight clinical decision support
  • Triage, remote consultation and prescribing where clinically appropriate
  • Tailored after an introductory conversation to ensure clinical suitability

Our clinicians

Experienced, calm and thorough.

Zinlea Night is delivered by experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services, trained in remote consultation safety and safeguarding.

Zinlea Night is a remote service. We do not provide home visits or in-person examinations, and we guide you to the right service when you need one.
