About Zinlea Night

Founding membership is currently limited while we onboard our first members.

Zinlea Night was created to bring calm, expert overnight medical advice to London during the hours when healthcare access can feel uncertain.

When you contact Zinlea Night, you speak directly with an experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioner. Consultations are unhurried: we listen carefully, ask the right questions and give clear clinical guidance.

Unlike a standard out-of-hours GP service, our focus is on continuity and reassurance. When in-person care is needed, we help you understand your options and escalate appropriately.

Our clinicians

Experienced, calm and thorough.

Zinlea Night is delivered by experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services. Our clinicians are trained in remote consultation safety, safeguarding and premium patient experience standards.

Zinlea Night is a remote service by design. We do not provide home visits or in-person examinations. When in-person assessment is required, we guide you to the appropriate service.
