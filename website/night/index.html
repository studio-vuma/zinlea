Our clinicians

Experienced, calm and thorough.

Zinlea Night is delivered by experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services, trained in remote consultation safety and safeguarding.

Zinlea Night is a remote service. We do not provide home visits or in-person examinations, and we guide you to the right service when you need one.

Zinlea Night is not an emergency service. If you or someone else has chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding or signs of stroke, call 999 immediately or go to your nearest A&E.
