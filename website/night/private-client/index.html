Care that knows you.

Some families want more than access to a clinician. They want a clinician who already knows the history, the medication and the people involved.

What Private Client includes.

Everything in Zinlea Night membership, with care arranged personally around you. The details are agreed with you during your consultation.

One point of contact

A named lead clinician

A senior clinician who knows you, your household and your medical history, and leads your care.

Specialist input

Specialist coordination

Referrals and liaison with specialist consultants and your own doctors, arranged on your behalf.

Written for you

A personal care plan

A plan that reflects your history and preferences, so every overnight call starts from what we already know.

No rush

Extended consultations

Longer, unhurried conversations and follow-up guidance after every call.

Your household

Family and staff

Care that can extend to family members and, where appropriate, household staff.

Complete discretion

Quiet by design

Strict confidentiality and personal boundaries, whoever you are.
