About Zinlea

What is Zinlea Health?

Zinlea Health is a private, clinician-led healthcare company. It is home to Zinlea Night, which provides overnight clinical advice in London, and Zinlea Global, which builds healthcare partnerships across Africa.

How do I get in touch?

Use our contact form or email info@zinlea.com. We reply to every enquiry within 24 hours.

Is Zinlea regulated?

Zinlea Night has submitted an application for registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), the independent regulator of health and social care in England. While the application is under review, Zinlea Night operates as a clinician-led private healthcare service. Read more on our clinical governance page.

How do you look after my information?

We operate in accordance with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and medical confidentiality standards. Patient information is stored securely, accessed only by authorised clinicians and used solely for care. See our privacy policy.

Zinlea Night

What is Zinlea Night?

A private membership providing overnight medical advice in London through experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners, from 8pm to 8am.

Who will I speak to?

You speak directly with an experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioner, with a background across urgent care, primary care or acute services.

How do consultations take place?

By telephone or video. Zinlea Night is a remote service by design: we do not provide home visits or in-person examinations. When you need in-person care, we guide you to the right service.

Can you prescribe medication?

Yes, where it is clinically appropriate and safe, after proper assessment. Some medications cannot be prescribed remotely, and controlled drugs and high-risk medicines may be restricted. Prescribing decisions remain at the clinician's professional discretion.

Is Zinlea Night an emergency service?

No. If you or someone else has chest pain, difficulty breathing, loss of consciousness, severe bleeding, signs of stroke or any life-threatening symptoms, call 999 immediately or go to your nearest A&E. Zinlea Night cannot dispatch ambulances.

Does Zinlea Night replace my GP?

No. Zinlea Night complements your registered GP, NHS urgent care and hospital services. We encourage members to stay in touch with their GP for ongoing and long-term needs.

How much does membership cost?

Individual membership starts from £85 a month and Family membership from £225 a month. Partnerships are tailored, and Private Client fees are agreed after a private consultation. See membership.

How do I become a member?

Request membership through our enquiry form. We reply within 24 hours, then arrange a brief onboarding call to understand your needs and confirm your membership.

What is Private Client?

Our most personal service: a named lead clinician, coordination with specialist consultants and your own doctors, and a care plan built around you. It is arranged individually after a private consultation. Learn more.

Do you work with care homes and organisations?

Yes. We partner with a small number of care homes, residences, family offices and organisations to provide overnight escalation support. About partnerships.

Zinlea Global

What is Zinlea Global?

Zinlea Global works with healthcare providers, clinicians and investors across Africa to develop new healthcare services together, as equal partners.

Is Zinlea Global operating yet?

Zinlea Global is in development. We are currently speaking with prospective founding partners.

Who can partner with Zinlea Global?

Hospital groups, private clinics, clinicians and investors. Register your interest and we will be in touch.
