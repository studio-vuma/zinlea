Zinlea Global · Africa

London standards. African ambition.

Zinlea Global brings Zinlea Health’s clinician-led, governance-first approach to Africa’s growing healthcare markets, built in partnership with the people who know them best.

The opportunity

Zinlea Global is in development, and we are speaking with prospective founding partners.

Across the continent, demand for private healthcare is growing quickly. We want to help partners meet it with care people can trust.

Our experience in London has shown how much calm expertise, clear escalation and strong clinical governance matter, especially out of hours.

Zinlea Global will bring those foundations to new markets, shaped with local clinicians and adapted to local needs, culture and regulation.

Our focus

Where we will add value.

Three areas where Zinlea’s experience can make a practical difference for partners.

  1. 01

    Provider partnerships

    Working alongside hospital groups and private clinics to strengthen services, including out-of-hours and remote clinical support.

  2. 02

    Clinical governance & training

    Sharing protocols, escalation pathways and training, so teams can grow safely and confidently.

  3. 03

    Models of care

    Designing sustainable, locally led service models with partners and investors.
