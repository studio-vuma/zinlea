Zinlea Global · Now speaking with founding partners

Healthcare partnerships, built together.

Zinlea Global works with healthcare providers, clinicians and investors across Africa to develop new services, as equal partners from the very first conversation.

Register interest What we build

Africa’s healthcare sector is home to outstanding clinicians, entrepreneurs and innovators. We want to build alongside them.

Why partnership

Each side brings what it knows best.

We bring our experience of running a private, clinician-led overnight service. Our partners bring their own expertise, relationships and deep understanding of their markets.

Together, we can develop services that neither of us could build alone, and learn from each other along the way.

What we can build together.

Three areas where we are looking to collaborate.

Hospitals & clinics

Provider partnerships

Working with hospital groups and private clinics to develop new services, including out-of-hours and remote clinical support.

Both directions

Shared learning

Exchanging clinical protocols, escalation pathways and training, with knowledge flowing in both directions.

Built to last

Models of care

Co-designing sustainable service models with partners and investors.

How we work

Partnership, from the first conversation.
