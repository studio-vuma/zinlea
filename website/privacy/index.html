Privacy policy

Your privacy matters. Here’s how we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: April 2026

Introduction

Zinlea Night (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our overnight clinical care services.

We are registered in England and Wales and act as the data controller for the personal information we process. We comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Information we collect

Personal information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Membership and account details
  • Partnership organisation details (where applicable)

Health information

  • Medical history and symptoms discussed during consultations
  • Clinical notes from nurse consultations
  • Treatment recommendations and guidance provided

Technical data

  • IP address, browser type, device information
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

How we use your information

  • Provide overnight clinical consultations and services
  • Communicate with you about your membership
  • Process partnership enquiries
  • Improve our services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

Legal basis for processing

  • Consent: when you explicitly agree to receive communications
  • Contract: when processing is necessary to provide clinical services you’ve requested
  • Legitimate interests: for business operations and service improvements
  • Legal obligation: when we must comply with healthcare regulations
  • Vital interests: in emergency situations

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data
  • Restrict processing: limit how we use your data
  • Data portability: receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: opt out of certain processing activities
  • Withdraw consent: remove previously given consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at info@zinlea.com.

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies on this website. Under UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), we only set non-essential cookies (such as analytics) after you have given us your explicit consent. Analytics cookies are blocked by default until you opt in.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@zinlea.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
