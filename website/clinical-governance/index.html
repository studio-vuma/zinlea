Regulation & service information

Clinical governance, regulation & service information

Transparency, safety and regulatory compliance are at the heart of everything we do.

Regulatory status

Zinlea Night has submitted an application for registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), the independent regulator of health and social care services in England.

We are committed to meeting all regulatory requirements and operating in accordance with recognised standards for safe, effective and well-governed healthcare.

While our application is under review, Zinlea Night operates as a clinician-led private healthcare service providing remote consultations, prescribing where appropriate, and personalised overnight care support.

Our service is designed to complement existing NHS and private healthcare provision.

Scope of service

Zinlea Night provides private remote consultations and personalised overnight clinical support between 8pm and 8am. Services may include:

  • Remote video or telephone consultations
  • Clinical advice and triage
  • Remote prescribing (where clinically appropriate)
  • Care planning and follow-up
  • Overnight reassurance and guidance
  • Coordination with other healthcare providers

All consultations are delivered by appropriately qualified clinicians working within UK clinical guidelines and professional standards.

Not an emergency service

Zinlea Night is not an emergency service and does not provide emergency medical response. If you or someone else experiences:

  • Chest pain
  • Difficulty breathing
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe bleeding
  • Signs of stroke
  • Any life-threatening symptoms

You must call 999 immediately or attend the nearest Accident & Emergency department. Zinlea Night cannot dispatch ambulances or provide emergency interventions.

Relationship to your GP & NHS services

Zinlea Night complements, but does not replace:

  • Your registered GP
  • NHS urgent care
  • Hospital services
  • Specialist medical care

Patients are encouraged to maintain contact with their GP for ongoing and long-term medical needs. Where appropriate, we may advise you to seek in-person care.

Clinical governance

Zinlea Night is establishing a comprehensive clinical governance framework in line with CQC expectations. This includes:

Clinical leadership

All clinicians are appropriately qualified, registered with relevant professional bodies, and work within defined clinical protocols.

Safe prescribing

Prescribing is undertaken only where clinically appropriate and following proper assessment. Prescribers operate in accordance with UK prescribing guidelines.

Risk management

  • Clear escalation procedures
  • Incident reporting
  • Clinical audit
  • Continuous service review

Safeguarding

We maintain safeguarding policies for both adults and children and follow established reporting procedures where concerns arise.

Quality assurance

We monitor:

  • Patient outcomes
  • Satisfaction
  • Safety incidents
  • Service performance

Prescribing policy

Zinlea Night offers remote prescribing where clinically appropriate and safe. However:

  • Not all conditions are suitable for remote prescribing
  • Some medications cannot be prescribed remotely
  • Clinicians may decline to prescribe if assessment is insufficient
  • Controlled drugs and high-risk medications may be restricted

Prescribing decisions remain entirely at the clinician’s professional discretion.

Suitability of remote care

Remote consultations are not appropriate for all medical situations. Where necessary, we may advise you to:

  • Seek in-person medical care
  • Contact your GP
  • Attend urgent care
  • Call emergency services

Zinlea Night reserves the right to decline consultation where remote care is not clinically suitable.

Personalised & concierge care

Zinlea Night provides a personalised and high-touch service designed for comfort, discretion and continuity. Our concierge-style model may include:

  • Extended consultation time
  • Follow-up guidance
  • Coordination with other services

This does not replace clinical judgement or regulatory standards. All care decisions remain based on medical need and safety.

Data protection & confidentiality

We are committed to protecting patient privacy and maintaining confidentiality. Zinlea Night operates in accordance with:

  • UK GDPR
  • Data Protection Act 2018
  • Medical confidentiality standards

Patient information is:

  • Stored securely
  • Accessed only by authorised clinicians
  • Used solely for care provision

Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details.

Patient responsibilities

By using Zinlea Night, you confirm that:

  • Information you provide is accurate
  • You understand the limits of remote care
  • You will seek emergency care when advised
  • You will maintain contact with your GP

Complaints & feedback

We are committed to continuous improvement and welcome feedback. Complaints can be submitted via info@zinlea.com. All complaints are reviewed through our governance procedures.
