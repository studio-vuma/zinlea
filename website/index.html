Who we are

Healthcare that’s there when you need it.

Zinlea Health exists to make expert healthcare easier to reach at the moments it matters most. We design services around the people who use them: calm, private and unhurried, with clinical safety at the centre of every decision.

Our first service, Zinlea Night, gives members in London direct access to experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners between 8pm and 8am. Through Zinlea Global, we are building healthcare partnerships with providers, clinicians and investors across Africa.

Zinlea Night

A senior nurse, at the end of the phone.

When you contact Zinlea Night, you speak directly with an experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioner. Consultations are unhurried: we listen carefully, ask the right questions and give clear clinical guidance.

  • Overnight access, 8pm to 8am, by phone or video
  • Medication guidance and remote prescribing where clinically appropriate
  • Clear escalation when you need in-person care
