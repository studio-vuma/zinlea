About Zinlea Health

One standard of care, delivered through two divisions.

We believe good healthcare should feel private, professional and unhurried, whatever the hour and wherever you are.

Zinlea Health brings together clinicians with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services. Our first service, Zinlea Night, gives Londoners calm, expert clinical advice during the hours when healthcare can feel hardest to reach.

Through Zinlea Global, we are building partnerships with healthcare providers, clinicians and investors across Africa, developing new services together as equal partners.
