About Zinlea Health

Clinician-led care, built on calm expertise.

Zinlea Health is a private healthcare company led by experienced clinicians. We believe healthcare should feel private, professional and unhurried, whatever the hour.

Our story

We started with the hardest hours.

Zinlea began with Zinlea Night, bringing calm, expert overnight medical advice to London during the hours when healthcare access can feel uncertain.

Through Zinlea Global, we are now building healthcare partnerships across Africa, working with providers, clinicians and investors as equal partners.

Good healthcare should feel private, professional and unhurried. Whatever the hour, wherever you are.

Our principles.

The same principles guide every Zinlea service.

How we speak to you

Calm expertise

Clinical advice delivered thoughtfully and without rush, by experienced clinicians who listen carefully.

How we treat your information

Discretion

Private care with complete respect for confidentiality and personal boundaries.

How we stay with you

Continuity

Relationships built over time, so you are supported consistently.

Our clinicians

Experienced, calm and thorough.

Our clinicians are experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services, trained in remote consultation safety, safeguarding and patient experience standards.
