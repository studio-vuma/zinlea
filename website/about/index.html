About Zinlea Health

Who we are.

Zinlea Health is a private, clinician-led healthcare company. We create services for the moments when expert care is hardest to reach, and we build them around calm expertise, discretion and continuity.

Our purpose

Expert care, without the wait or the worry.

Most healthcare is designed around opening hours. Worry isn’t. Zinlea exists so that people can speak to an experienced clinician when they need one, and come away with a clear answer about what to do next.

We are a private company, led by clinicians, and we work alongside the NHS and your own GP rather than replacing them.

What we do.

Zinlea Health is the parent company of two services, each with its own focus.

London · 20:00 – 08:00Zinlea NightPrivate overnight medical advice. Members speak directly to experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners by phone or video, with remote prescribing where clinically appropriate and clear escalation when in-person care is needed. Zinlea Night also supports care homes, residences and family offices. Across Africa · In developmentZinlea GlobalHealthcare partnerships with providers, clinicians and investors across Africa, developing new services together as equal partners, with knowledge flowing in both directions.

Good healthcare should feel private, professional and unhurried. Whatever the hour, wherever you are.

How we work.

The same principles guide every Zinlea service, from a single overnight call to a new partnership.

How we speak to you

Calm expertise

Clinical advice delivered thoughtfully and without rush, by experienced clinicians who listen carefully.

How we treat your information

Discretion

Private care with complete respect for confidentiality and personal boundaries.

How we stay with you

Continuity

Relationships built over time, so you are supported consistently.

How we keep you safe

Safety first

Clear escalation pathways, and honesty about what remote care can and cannot do.

How we are governed

Accountability

Clinical protocols, incident reporting, clinical audit and continuous service review.

How we work with others

Partnership

We complement the NHS, your GP and our partners, and we coordinate with them when needed.

Our clinicians

Experienced, calm and thorough.

Our clinicians are experienced Advanced Nurse Practitioners with backgrounds across urgent care, primary care and acute services. They are registered with their professional bodies, work within defined clinical protocols, and are trained in remote consultation safety, safeguarding and patient experience standards.

Regulation and safety

Transparent about where we are.

Zinlea Night has submitted an application for registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), the independent regulator of health and social care in England. While our application is under review, we operate as a clinician-led private healthcare service, in line with UK clinical guidelines and professional standards.
