Selected work

Professional experience and independent research.

Client confidentiality applies to commissioned work. What follows is described accurately: professional experience, research in progress and independent study are marked as such.

Professional experience

Retail strategy: from shopfront to online

For a London retailer weighing the future of its physical store, I analysed search demand and competitor activity to understand where customers were really looking. The analysis informed the decision to move the business from its physical store to online retail.

Where a business chooses to be present is a decision for evidence rather than instinct.

Professional experience

A partnership conversation with Forbes Africa

While building the public profile of a new practice focused on African luxury strategy, I wrote long-form editorial and led media and partnership outreach. Forbes Africa requested a partnership call, for which I prepared the partnership deck.

A clear point of view, expressed with care, opens doors that promotion alone does not.

Research in progress

Madagascan caviar and luxury hospitality in Marrakech

A mixed-methods study, built around structured interviews with hotel food and beverage managers, examining how Madagascan caviar could be distributed through luxury hotels in Marrakech.

How an African luxury product might reach international luxury hospitality on its own terms.

Independent study, not client work

The Zambia Luxury Lodge Collection

An independent examination of how four distinct Zambian safari lodges, brought under one owner-led brand in 2026, might become one choice in the minds of international travellers, and of the case for conservation as the collection's unifying idea.

Becoming known for something specific is a choice that has to show in every detail.
