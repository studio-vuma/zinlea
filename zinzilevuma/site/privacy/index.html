Privacy notice

This notice explains how Zinzile Vuma uses the personal information you share through this website.

What is collected

When you send an enquiry, the details you enter are collected: your name, role, organisation, email address, area of interest, markets of interest and message. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How it is used

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and, if we go on to work together, to correspond with you about that work. They are not added to marketing lists and are never sold or shared for marketing.

Where it is stored

Enquiries are received through the website's hosting provider, Netlify, and by email. Information is kept only for as long as it is needed for these purposes.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you at any time by writing to enquiries@zinzilevuma.com.
