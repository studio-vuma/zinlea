About Zinzile
"Many brands are replicating the European model of luxury. I believe African luxury can build its own brand culture."

I'm Zinzile Vuma, a London-based luxury brand advisor with a background in luxury brand management and a specific focus on African luxury.

African brands already have the heritage, the craft and the story. They don't need to borrow someone else's codes to be valued. My work is helping them define what African luxury means, and carry it confidently into international markets.

Governments and institutions are moving towards provenance, and I believe origin, craft and place are among the most powerful assets an African brand can build on.

My background spans the UK and Southern Africa, and my approach to clients is shaped by years of looking after premium clients in London: understanding what they choose, return for and recommend.

Retail strategy

Knowing where customers really look

Professional work

A shop is only as strong as the place its customers go looking for it.

The situation
A London retailer was weighing the future of its physical store.
What I did
I studied where demand for its products actually sat: what customers were searching for, how often, how hard each search was to win and what they meant to do next. Alongside that, I followed how competitors were meeting the same demand.
What changed
The analysis informed the decision to move the business from its physical store to online retail. I also created a blog template that became the standard for its future publications.

What it showsWhere a brand chooses to be seen is a decision for evidence, not instinct. It is the same thinking I bring to choosing which market a brand enters first.

Market research

Madagascan caviar, at the tables of Marrakech

Research in progress

Can an African luxury product reach international luxury hospitality on its own terms?

The question
How could Madagascan caviar be distributed through luxury hotels in Marrakech?
The approach
A mixed-methods study built around structured interviews with hotel food and beverage managers, the people who decide what reaches the table.
Why it matters
Origin is often the most valuable thing an African product has, and the first thing lost on the way to market. This study asks how to carry it intact.

What it showsI go to the people who make the buying decisions and listen before forming a view. Every engagement starts the same way.

Brand study

Four lodges, one brand

Independent study, not client work

A collection becomes one choice when a single idea runs through every stay.

The subject
The Zambia Luxury Lodge Collection, which in 2026 brought four Zambian safari lodges under one owner-led brand.
The question
How does a collection of distinct lodges become one choice in the minds of international travellers?
My perspective
Lead with one idea that every guest feels, wherever they stay. Here, conservation could be that thread: not an add-on, but the reason the collection exists.

What it showsBecoming known for something specific is a choice, and it has to show in every detail, from the first enquiry to the drive back to the airstrip.
