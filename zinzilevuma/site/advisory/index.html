Scope

Disciplines, not packages.

These disciplines describe the areas in which I advise. They are not fixed products.

Many engagements draw on two or three. A decision about entering a market may depend on positioning and provenance; a destination strategy may need research before anything else. We agree the scope together, once I understand the question you are trying to answer, and it is set out in a written proposal before any work begins.

How I work

We work on the problem together.

Clients do not hand me a brief and wait for a report. The most useful strategy comes from combining what you know about your business with an independent view from outside it.

What you bring

Knowledge of the business, its ambitions and its operating realities, and the commercial objectives and constraints any recommendation must respect.

What I bring

Independent research and market intelligence, critical analysis, an external perspective, and an understanding of how luxury and international markets work.

What we do together

Examine the problem, challenge assumptions, weigh the alternatives and arrive at a strategic direction you can act on with confidence.

  • Direct engagement with me throughout
  • A clear understanding of your objectives and circumstances
  • Perspectives developed together, with assumptions tested openly
  • Independent research and critical analysis
  • Careful consideration of commercial implications
  • Clear strategic recommendations and direction
  • Continued advisory support where it is useful
