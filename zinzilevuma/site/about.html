About & Work

An African point of view on luxury.

About Zinzile
"Many brands are replicating the European model of luxury. I believe African luxury can build its own brand culture."

I'm Zinzile Vuma, a London-based luxury brand advisor with a background in luxury brand management and a specific focus on African luxury.

African brands already have the heritage, the craft and the story. They don't need to borrow someone else's codes to be valued. My work is helping them define what African luxury means, and carry it confidently into international markets.

Governments and institutions are moving towards provenance, and I believe origin, craft and place are among the most powerful assets an African brand can build on.

My background spans the UK and Southern Africa, and my approach to clients is shaped by years of looking after premium clients in London: understanding what they choose, return for and recommend.

Work & Studies

Strategy grounded in research, customers and markets.

Retail Strategy · Placement

From shopfront to online

The context
A London retailer weighing the future of its physical store.
What I did
Keyword research and competitor monitoring in SEMrush, reading search demand by volume, difficulty and intent to find where customers were really looking.
The outcome
The analysis informed the decision to move the business from a physical store to online retail. I also created an SEO blog template adopted as the standard for future publications.
Partnerships

Opening the door to Forbes Africa

The context
Building the public profile of a new African luxury strategy practice.
What I did
Media and partnership outreach, supported by long-form editorial on African luxury strategy.
The outcome
Forbes Africa requested a partnership call, and I developed the partnership deck for that conversation.
Market Research

Caviar, from Madagascar to Marrakech

Research in progress
The question
How could Madagascan caviar be distributed through luxury hotels in Marrakech?
The approach
A mixed-methods study built around structured interviews with hotel food and beverage managers.
Why it matters
It explores how an African luxury product can reach international luxury hospitality on its own terms.
Independent Brand Study

Four lodges, one brand

Independent study · Not client work
The subject
The Zambia Luxury Lodge Collection, which in 2026 brought four Zambian safari lodges under one owner-led brand.
The question
How does a collection of distinct lodges become one choice in the minds of international travellers?
My perspective
Lead with a single idea that runs through every stay. Here, conservation could be that thread: not an add-on, but the reason the collection exists.
