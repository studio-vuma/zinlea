My approach

To a client, luxury is ease: the feeling that every detail has been considered. Nothing has to be explained twice and nothing feels left to chance. That is what I help brands give their customers, and it starts long before anything is in someone’s hands.

Provenance, defined before it is shown

Provenance is a popular word among African brands, but few brands know how to express it. Naming where something comes from is not enough. It has to be defined first: what the origin means for this brand, and why it matters to the person buying. Then it has to show consistently in how the brand looks, speaks and presents itself.

Known for something specific

South Africa and Kenya are known for game drives. The Seychelles are known for their beaches. A brand, and the region it comes from, should become known for something just as specific. That clarity is what people remember, and what they are prepared to pay for.

In its own right

African brands do not need to copy someone else’s rule book. They can build this in their own right, from their own heritage and craft, and they can go further than they expect.
