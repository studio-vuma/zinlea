Perspective

From London, with a pan-African and international view.

London is the base of the practice. Its perspective is pan-African and global.

I am interested in the relationship between African commercial opportunity and international markets, in both directions: African producers, brands and destinations building positions in Europe, the Gulf and North America, and international brands, investors and organisations considering African markets.

Sectors of focus

  • Luxury and premium goods
  • Hospitality, tourism and destinations
  • Origin-led food and drink
  • Craft, design and the creative industries
  • Retail and distribution
  • Public institutions and sector bodies

Who I advise

  • Founders and owners
  • CEOs and executive leadership
  • Investors and commercial stakeholders
  • Corporations and international brands
  • African producers and businesses
  • Governments and public institutions
  • Tourism and destination authorities
