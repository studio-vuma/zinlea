Questions and answers

What people usually ask before we speak.

Who do you work with?

Founders and directors of brands built on heritage, craftsmanship and a story worth telling. That includes makers, hospitality, food and drink producers, fashion, fragrance and beauty labels, and family businesses with real history.

If you are unsure whether your business fits, the 30-minute call is the place to find out.

Do you only work with African brands?

No. I work with brands of all kinds. My specialism is provenance for African companies, so that is where much of my work sits.

What happens on the 30-minute call?

I ask about your business: what you make, who buys it, where you want to take it and what is in the way. It is not an advice session. Its purpose is for me to understand what you would want from working together.

If it makes sense to continue, I will suggest the next step.

What is the assessment?

A close look at the brand as it stands: how it looks, speaks, presents itself and prices what it makes. I come back with what I see, what I would change first and a proposal for the work.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the scope of the work. I set them out clearly in the proposal that follows the assessment, before any work begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on what we agree to do. The proposal sets out the timing alongside the fee.

What do you mean by provenance?

Where a brand comes from, and what that origin means to the person buying. I help brands define it first, then show it consistently in how they look, speak and present themselves.

Do you design logos and packaging?

I set the direction for the identity and work alongside your designers so the result holds together.

Do you help with sourcing or production?

No. My work is the brand: its story, provenance, identity, positioning and pricing.

Why do you take on so few clients?

Because the work needs attention. Every client of mine is a luxury client and receives the same care I help them give their customers.

Where are you based?

London. I work with clients remotely, wherever they are.

Something else?

Ask me directly. I am happy to answer before we arrange a call.
