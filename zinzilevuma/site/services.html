Services

I work with brands whose value rests on where they come from, how their things are made and the story behind them. Their customers are discerning. They notice the stitching, ask who made the piece and want to know why it is worth what it costs.

To a client like that, luxury is ease: the feeling that every detail has been considered. My work is to make sure the brand gives that feeling every time, in how it looks, speaks, presents itself and prices what it makes.

Who I work with

Most of the businesses I work with fall into one of these groups. If yours does not, the call is still a good place to find out whether I can help.

Makers and craft-led brands
Leather, textiles, jewellery, ceramics, furniture and homeware. Usually looking for a brand story that does justice to the craft, and pricing that reflects it.
Hospitality
Lodges, safari camps, boutique hotels and private villas. Usually looking to be known for something specific to their place, and to say it consistently from the website to the welcome.
Food and drink producers
Wine estates, coffee, tea, spirits and cacao. Usually looking to define their provenance clearly and present it to buyers abroad.
Fashion, fragrance and beauty
Labels built on local materials, ingredients or techniques. Usually looking for positioning and an identity that can stand next to established international names.
Family businesses and established names
Companies with real heritage that has never been written down, or has been told inconsistently. Usually preparing for a new generation, a new market or a relaunch.

What I do

Brand story and heritage

I find and write the story that is already in the business: its origins, its people, its place and its craft. The result is a clear account of who you are that your team, your stockists and your customers can repeat.

Suits makers, family businesses and anyone whose story is strong but scattered.

Provenance

Provenance is a popular word among African brands, but few brands know how to express it. I help you define what your origin actually means, then make sure it shows consistently in how the brand looks, speaks and presents itself.

Suits African companies, especially producers, makers and hospitality brands tied to a particular region.

Brand identity

How the brand looks and sounds: name, visual direction, tone of voice and the way it presents itself in person, in print and online. I set the direction and work alongside your designers so the result holds together.

Suits new brands, and established ones that have outgrown their first identity.

Positioning, scarcity and pricing

Where the brand sits, who it is for and what it should become known for. This includes how rarity and price tell the same story: what to make in small numbers, what to keep available, and how to price so the value is clear rather than claimed.

Suits brands entering new markets, raising prices, or launching limited pieces or collections.

Ongoing advisory

A standing arrangement for decisions as they come up: a launch, a collaboration, a new market, a change in price. You have someone who knows the brand well and can give a considered view quickly.

Suits founders and directors after a first project, or those who want a steady second opinion.

How we start

  1. A free 30-minute callI ask about your business: what you make, who buys it, where you want to take it and what is in the way. The call is for me to understand what you need, not to give advice. By the end we will both know whether working together makes sense.

  3. AdvisoryWe work through the agreed project together. Afterwards, some clients keep me on to advise as new decisions come up.
