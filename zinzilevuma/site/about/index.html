Perspective

What I believe, and why it shapes the work.

Luxury as commercial architecture

My interest in luxury has never been confined to its surface. What draws me is its architecture: how perception, scarcity, craft, price and access are arranged so that something is understood as exceptional and valued accordingly. To a client, luxury is ease, the feeling that every detail has been considered. Behind that ease sits a series of deliberate commercial decisions. That is where my work begins.

Origin and value

I am particularly concerned with the relationship between origin and value. African raw materials, techniques and cultural ideas underpin some of the world's most valued goods and experiences, yet much of that value is realised elsewhere. Provenance is now a popular word, but few businesses know how to express it, and fewer still how to own it. It has to be defined first, then shown consistently and protected.

African enterprise, in its own right

I believe African businesses, destinations and institutions can build distinctive positions in international markets on their own terms, rather than following someone else's rule book. A brand and its region can become known for something specific, the way some destinations are known around the world for a single experience. They can go further than they expect.

Beyond conventional metrics

Markets are not fully described by their size. I look at the competitive landscape, the behaviour of buyers, the commercial and institutional conditions, and above all at what is beginning to change. The aim is to help clients understand where markets are, anticipate where they are going and position themselves for what comes next.

An independent practice

My practice is deliberately personal. Clients work with me directly, from the first conversation onwards. I take on a limited number of engagements at a time so that each receives senior attention. Every client is treated as a luxury client, and receives the same care I help them give their own customers.

Background

Training and experience.

I hold a BA (Hons) in Luxury Brand Management from Regent's University London, and my professional background is in luxury brand management. My approach to clients has been shaped by years of looking after premium clients in London: understanding what they choose, what they return for and what they recommend. My background spans the United Kingdom and Southern Africa.
