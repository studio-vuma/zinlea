Portfolio  /  Case 01

Advisory portfolio · Case 01 · Academic placement

Carrying a heritage forward, online.

A curated multi-brand platform for independent British designers had built its following through pop-up spaces. Each location drew a different crowd and a different set of supporters. The next part of their story was taking that heritage, and the following the pop-ups had built, online without losing the craft and storytelling that set them apart.

Client
Curated multi-brand platform for independent British designers
Sector
Contemporary womenswear: curated, no-waste, organic British fashion
My role
SEO and digital discovery strategy

Focus
Editorial, search visibility and trend mapping

1Chapter one · Where it began

Between editorial and retail.

The platform brings together independent British designers, with a focus on curated, no-waste, organic fashion, much of it made to order.

  • Curated
  • No-waste
  • Organic
  • Made to order

Its designers came to the platform because of its pop-ups. Many of them already sold online through their own channels, but the pop-ups gave them a physical space and a following in each location.

Until then, most of the selling happened in person. Customers could touch the garments and understand how they were made, and that physical experience was what everyone leaned towards, the designers included.

I worked directly with the founders throughout.

2Chapter two · The crossroads

From the room to the screen.

The brief was to move from physical to digital discovery.

One goal mattered more than most. The designers had joined for the pop-ups, and many were already online on their own. The move had to give them a reason to want to be featured online through the platform too, as something that added to their presence rather than competed with it.

From

Physical presence

  • Pop-ups in different locations, each with its own crowd
  • Customers touching the garments to understand them
  • Designers drawn in by the physical space
→
To

Digital discovery

  • An online home for curated British fashion
  • The craft and the story, explained so customers can find it
  • Designers who see being featured online as a plus
3Chapter three · Finding the way

Built around the brand, not a template.

I didn't use a standard marketing playbook. I started with the founders' goals and the brand's identity, and shaped every recommendation around them.

The work had four stages.

  1. 1

    Listen

    I sat down with the founders to understand their goals, the culture they had built and the many designer identities living under one roof.

  2. 2

    Map

    I read seasonal runway and industry trend signals, and matched them to stories the brand was already telling: craft, texture, layering and material heritage.

  3. 3

    Structure

    I built a keyword and editorial framework covering blog themes, styling guidance and tone of voice.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    I left a repeatable system the team can keep using, so nobody starts from a blank page each season.

4Chapter four · The customer's path

From inspiration to purchase.

The framework gives a customer a clear path through the site, without turning a discovery platform into a hard-sell shop.

  1. Search

    Found through considered keywords

  2. Read

    Editorial and designer stories

  3. Style

    Guidance on how to wear it

  4. Buy

    A considered purchase

5Chapter five · The tension

Visibility without dilution.

Search visibility mattered a great deal, but chasing it too hard would flatten a no-waste, story-led brand. The strategy had to balance both.

Search visibilityBrand identity

Balanced: keywords that support the storytelling rather than replace it.

6Chapter six · What changed

Value created.

The work strengthened the client in four areas.

Strategic

A real move online

Supported the shift away from reliance on pop-ups towards a stronger digital discovery platform.

Brand

Positioning reinforced

Strengthened the client's place as a curated destination for contemporary British design.

Digital

Clearer navigation

The keyword and editorial structure created clear routes across styling guidance and blog pages.

Commercial

Browsing to buying

Moved visitors from inspiration-led browsing to structured engagement, with the identity intact.

7Chapter seven · The road ahead

Where I would take it next.

  1. iCompetitor keyword benchmarking, to see where the client can own search ground.
  2. iiClear performance measures showing how editorial content turns into engagement and sales over time.
  3. iiiA plan to reassure partner designers that moving online adds to their presence rather than taking from it.
