Where it began

A multi-brand platform built on pop-ups.

A curated platform for independent British designers had built its following through pop-up spaces. Each location drew a different crowd and a different set of supporters.

CuratedNo-wasteOrganicMade to order

Customers came to touch the garments and understand how they were made. That physical experience was what everyone leaned towards, the designers included.

The real goal

Bring the designers with us.

The designers had joined for the pop-ups, and many already sold online on their own.

The move online had to give them a reason to want to be featured there through the platform too, as something that added to their presence rather than competed with it.

Working directly with the founders

The approach

Built around the brand, not a template.

  1. 1

    Listen

    The founders' goals, the culture they had built, and many designer identities under one roof.

  2. 2

    Map

    Runway and industry trend signals, matched to stories the brand already told.

  3. 3

    Structure

    A keyword and editorial framework: blog themes, styling guidance, tone of voice.

  4. 4

    Hand over

    A repeatable system, so nobody starts from a blank page each season.
