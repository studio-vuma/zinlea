Zinlea Global · Africa

London standards. African ambition.

Zinlea Global brings the experience of Zinlea Health to Africa’s fast-growing healthcare markets — partnering with local providers to build trusted, sustainable models of care.

The opportunity

Growing demand deserves dependable care.

Across the continent, private healthcare is expanding quickly. Zinlea Global exists to help partners grow with confidence — combining proven clinical governance with deep respect for local expertise, culture and need.

  • Locally led — services shaped with, and run by, local teams.
  • Proven foundations — governance and ways of working tested in London.
  • Long-term partnership — we invest in relationships, not one-off projects.
  • Two-way learning — knowledge flows in both directions between markets.
Our focus

Where Zinlea Global adds value.

Provider partnerships

Working with hospital groups and private clinics to strengthen and extend services, including out-of-hours and overnight care.

Workforce & training

Sharing clinical standards, protocols and training to support teams as they grow.

Models of care

Designing service models with partners and investors that are safe, sustainable and built for local context.
